# Executive Memorandum: Growth Drivers, Retention, and Logistics Optimization

**To:** Leadership Team & Commercial Stakeholders  
**From:** Data Analytics Team  
**Date:** March 2026  
**Subject:** Marketplace Performance Analysis (2017–2018 Brazilian Commercial Dataset)

---

### Executive Overview
Between January 2017 and August 2018, gross marketplace sales expanded five-fold to reach **R$ 13.18 million** across **96,211 delivered orders**. However, underlying customer repeat rates remained completely stalled, and delivery delays severely damaged brand equity. This memorandum highlights three headline findings, three high-impact operational interventions, and key data boundaries.

---

### Headline Findings

* **Customer Retention is Stalled at 3.0% (The 30-Day Floor):**  
  Out of 93,104 unique customers, only 2,789 (3.00%) completed more than one purchase. Month 1 retention drops to **0.48%**, falls to 0.26% in Month 2, and asymptotes to noise thereafter. If a shopper does not repurchase within 30 days of delivery, the probability they ever return is under 0.3%.

* **Delivery Delays Halve Customer Satisfaction (-1.72 Stars):**  
  On-time deliveries average **4.29 out of 5 stars** (62.5% 5-star, 6.6% 1-star). When an order is delivered past the promised date, average customer ratings plummet to **2.57 stars**, with 1-star reviews surging to **46.2%**. This dissatisfaction cliff is non-linear and acute.

* **Revenue Follows a Broad Long-Tail, Not an 80/20 VIP Curve:**  
  The top 20% of buyers generate only **56.6% of revenue** (far below the traditional 80% rule). Reaching 80% of revenue requires **45.0% of all customers**. The marketplace thrives on high-volume, single-transaction customer acquisitions rather than a concentrated high-ticket VIP tier.

### Recommended Strategic Interventions

| Priority | Recommended Action | Functional Owner | Target Metric to Move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Reallocate Loyalty Budget to 30-Day Replenishment** | Cancel plans for a "Top 20% VIP Loyalty Program". Reallocate capital into automated replenishment reminders and category-specific discounts at 30, 45, and 60 days post-delivery for consumable categories (*Health & Beauty*, *Pet Shop*). | **Marketing & CRM** | Increase 60-day repeat purchase rate from **3.0% to 5.0%** (+R$ 260k GMV lift). |
| **2. Enforce Strict Merchant Dispatch SLAs** | Implement automated penalties and Buy Box suppression for high-volume merchants with $>15\%$ late dispatch rates. Focus on the 20 worst sellers who currently average 28–40 days delay. | **Merchant Operations** | Reduce platform late delivery rate from **8.0% to <5.0%**, directly preventing 1-star review surges. |
| **3. Pilot Regional Fulfillment Hubs in the Northeast** | Partner with regional consolidation carriers in Bahia or Pernambuco to store fast-moving inventory locally, cutting delivery transit times in Northern states (currently 24–30 days) down to under 10 days. | **Logistics & Supply Chain** | Reduce Alagoas late delivery rate from **23.99% to <10%** and lower freight burden from **55% to <35%**. |

---

### What This Analysis Does Not Tell Us

* **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** The dataset does not track paid advertising spend, channel attribution, or conversion funnels; we cannot calculate true customer acquisition efficiency or Lifetime Value to CAC (LTV:CAC) ratios.
* **Pre-Fulfillment Intent & Cart Drop-Off:** Records only exist for orders that were placed and completed; we cannot observe shoppers who abandoned carts due to 50–60% freight friction in remote states.
* **Causal Attribution:** While matched-pair analysis confirms delay creates an independent rating penalty, true mathematical causality requires controlled A/B testing with randomized shipping subsidies.
